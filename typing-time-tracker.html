Windows Typing Time Tracker

TypingWatch is a lightweight Windows typing time tracker designed to help users monitor typing activity, WPM speed and progress toward a typing-time goal. It supports separate Hindi and English typing totals and keeps the important information visible in a small desktop dashboard.

Download TypingWatch for Windows

Track Hindi and English typing time

For users who practice both Hindi and English typing, TypingWatch provides separate typing totals and daily tracking. This can be useful for regular typing practice, office work and typing-test preparation.

Monitor WPM and typing progress

The TypingWatch dashboard includes a WPM meter, typing-time information and progress toward a long-term typing goal. A speed warning can also help users notice when typing speed drops below their chosen level.

Typing practice and exam preparation

TypingWatch is a time tracker rather than a replacement for a typing-test website. You can use it alongside Hindi typing practice, English typing practice, WPM tests and exam-style typing passages to keep a record of your practice time.

Windows typing tracker features

  • Daily and total typing time

  • Typing-time goal tracking
